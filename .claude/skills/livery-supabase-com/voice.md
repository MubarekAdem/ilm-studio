# Voice

Tone: concise, impersonal, energetic, conversational.

- Keep sentences around 10 words; that is the measured average across the site's copy.
- Write headings in sentence case; only 3 of 10 measured headings use Title Case.
- Exclamation marks appear in the copy; use them sparingly for real moments of energy.
- Label buttons with short nouns or phrases, about 2 words long.

## Examples (new sentences in this voice)

- **headline**: Plan less. Ship more.
- **button**: Start now
- **empty state**: Nothing here yet.
- **error**: That didn't work. Try again.
- **headline**: Your best work starts today!
- **button**: Let's go
